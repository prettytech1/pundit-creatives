FAQ

Questions before you book.

Don’t see yours? Ask us directly on WhatsApp.

How much does a shoot cost?

Pricing depends on scope, location and deliverables. Send a quick brief on WhatsApp and you’ll get a clear, itemised quote within 48 hours.

How long until I get my video?

Reels are usually delivered 5–7 working days after the shoot. Brand films take 2–3 weeks. Event highlights can be turned around the same week.

Do you shoot outside Abuja?

Yes. We work across Nigeria. Travel and accommodation are included in your quote.

How do I secure a date?

Approve your quote and pay a deposit. Your date is then reserved.

What do I need to prepare?

Just your goal, your audience and any references you like. We handle the concept, shot list, crew and gear.

Can I get the raw footage?

Final edited files are included in every package. Raw footage can be added on request.
